How to Calculate a Pay Raise

Calculate the dollar and percentage impact of a salary increase.

Formula

New Salary = Current Salary × (1 + Raise %)

Example

A $60,000 salary with a 5% raise becomes $63,000. The annual increase is $3,000.

How to use the result

This calculation is useful for compensation comparisons, budgeting, payroll planning, and understanding the relationship between different forms of pay. The most useful result is one based on realistic working hours, pay periods, and compensation assumptions.

Practical tips

  • Compare both percentage and dollar change.
  • For monthly budgeting, divide the annual increase by 12.
  • Gross salary changes do not equal take-home pay changes.

Important limitation

Payroll calculations can be affected by employment agreements, employer policies, tax rules, benefit structures, and local laws. PayrollMetricsLab provides educational estimates and general information only.
